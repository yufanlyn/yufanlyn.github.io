About

Building humanoid robots and dexterous hands designed to genuinely benefit humanity.

Hi! I'm Yufan Lin, an undergraduate student pursuing a B.S. in Computer Science and Mathematics at the University of Massachusetts Amherst.

I currently work as a Research Assistant at the Huzhou Institute of Zhejiang University, focusing on humanoid robots and dexterous robotic hands. I am deeply grateful to Guannan Li for his mentorship and guidance.
